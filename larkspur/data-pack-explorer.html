Larkspur Foods at a glance

A family-controlled US food company with three divisions, twelve sites and about 60 contracted growers. The pack holds 16 documents and 4 data tables the client gave Windward. Everything in it is simulated course data. Read from top to bottom: this page, then the themes, then the raw files.

What's in the pack, grouped into six themes

Every file sits in one home theme. Click a card to open the summary, key numbers and questions for that theme.

Suggested reading order

  1. Company overview and org chart: who they are and who signs what.
  2. Board minutes: what leadership is worried about right now (insurance renewal, lender ESG questionnaire, a deferred expansion).
  3. Reference: the detailed numbers by product line, customer and inventory.
  4. The map, then the facilities and grower tables: where everything physically is.
  5. Water & permits: the thread running through plants and growers.
  6. The contracts (growers, co-packer, retailer, credit, energy, insurance): who carries the loss when something goes wrong. The supply theme compares the four grower contract forms side by side.

Company sites come from facilities-register.csv, which gives their coordinates. Growers are plotted at the town named in grower-network.csv.
